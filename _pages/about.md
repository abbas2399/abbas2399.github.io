I'm Abbas Muzammil — you can call me Abbas.

My journey into tech started simply by fixing broken computers. One thing led to another, and my curiosity drove me to study Computer Science, eventually going deeper into the field by earning my Master's in CS at Cleveland State University.

Today, I'm a software engineer at Flusso, working on BG Supply, a Flusso project. BG Supply is a Marketplace for the kitchen and bath industry where showroom owners and dealers can browse products from different manufacturers, create quotes, and send them—all in one platform. With a catalog of over 1M products, we integrate manufacturer data and use tools like Typesense for fast product discovery, supported by robust data engineering pipelines. My interests also include UI/UX, Robotics, and Mobile App Development.

If you're exploring a research problem, sitting on an idea that won't leave you alone, or looking for someone to build a business with — I'd love to talk. The best work I've seen happens when curious people find each other, and I'm always glad to start that conversation.

Beyond code, I enjoy calisthenics, music, travelling, playing badminton, and playing the piano! :)
